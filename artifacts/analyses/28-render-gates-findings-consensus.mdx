## Consensus ρ

Fix in this PR: **B8, N2, N8, N9 (hybrid), N12**.
Defer to follow-up issues: **B4, N3, N10, N11, N13**.
Skip as non-issues: **N4**.

### Rationale

Threat model is a build-time CLI tool used by trusted developers locally. Determinism is the project's load-bearing invariant — async-settle races (B8) and path-traversal in CI fork-PR contexts (N2) directly threaten it. Test correctness gaps (N8, N9, N12) are cheap to fix and prevent silent regressions. The deferred items (cli.ts split, sandbox gating, hermetic fixtures, integration smoke, --verbose) are real but bounded; they don't gate correctness and ship-blocking would prolong an iteration that is already on round 2.

### Trade-offs

1. Deferring B4 accepts the static-import cost on `lint` startup; acceptable until benchmarks show regression.
2. Deferring N11 bets that B5 (now landed) covers the meaningful correctness surface via real jsdom tests; the wrapper-level smoke test moves to a follow-up.
3. Skipping N4 codifies that DoS-only locals on developer-controlled input are acceptable risk in this threat model.
